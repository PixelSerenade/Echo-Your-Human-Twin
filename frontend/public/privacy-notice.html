Echo Privacy Notice

Echo uses the information you choose to share to provide personalized replies. Permission categories can be turned on or off in Privacy settings, and a disabled category is not read for personalized context.

When you ask Echo for a reply, relevant shared information and your request may be processed by Google Gemini to generate that reply.

For live information such as weather and holidays, Echo searches the web using only the public part of your question. Personal details and saved information are not included in public search queries.

Echo records each permission choice with its category, on/off value, wording version, and timestamp so you can review when a choice was made.

You can change your permission choices at any time in the Privacy screen. Changing your persona changes category examples only and does not change saved permission choices.
